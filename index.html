About

I am a graduate student in the Programming Language Research Group at KAIST, advised by Sukyoung Ryu. My research focuses on mechanizing programming language definitions to improve their reliability and precision. I lead the P4-SpecTec project, a language mechanization framework for P4. P4-SpecTec is now (as of August 2026) conditionally adopted as the official P4 specification authoring toolchain! Previously, I contributed to the Wasm-SpecTec project, also part of the official WebAssembly specification authoring toolchain. My goal is to make language specifications reliable, robustly maintained, and better aligned with the needs of the developer community.
